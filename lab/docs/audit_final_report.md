the raw results. A separate skeptic per dimension then tried to refute each finding. 72 findings were raised and 65
confirmed (several overlap across dimensions). All 65 were adopted in REPORT.md. Each entry gives the quoted text,
the problem, the verified replacement and the skeptic's reasoning.
