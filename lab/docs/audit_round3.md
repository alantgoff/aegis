### [4] new-baselines-h6 (major)
QUOTE: A ±30% task-mixture mismatch doubles the false-positive rate, to 10%.
PROBLEM: This claims more than the evidence shows. The 5% baseline is in-sample by construction: the threshold is the 95th percentile of the same 80 benign scores that are then re-scored. Paired, the change from 4/80 to 8/80 is not significant. At the detector's dev operating point it nearly disappears. A plain re-draw of the correct sampler moves the false-positive rate and median about as much as the mismatch does. The 8/80 figure is right, but 'doubles' presents a non-significant, threshold-dependent change as a reliable loss of specificity.
FINAL: A ±30% task-mixture mismatch gives a false-positive rate of 8/80 (10%) at the 5%-FPR threshold set on the same benign models, against 4/80 by construction under the correct sampler (exact McNemar p = 0.13; 7/80 vs 8/80 at the dev threshold), so a loss of specificity is not established.
WHY: Confirmed independently. h6.py:64-65 sets the threshold to the 95th percentile of the same 80 clean+extra tug_frozen scores (0.1564), so 4/80 lie above it by construction. My paired recount (/tmp/claude-0/audit3/sk_nb/h6pair.py): under the task mismatch 8/80 are flagged, 4 by both samplers, 4 only under the mismatch, 0 only under the correct sampler; exact McNemar p = 0.125. At the dev threshold 0.146 the count is 7/80 under the correct sampler and 8/80 under the mismatch (1 discordant, p = 1.0). The scratch re-seed control checks out (/tmp/claude-0/audit3/sk_nb/reseedchk.py). The correct sampler at seed 778 flags 5/80, median 0.084, Spearman 0.91 with seed 777. Mismatch vs that re-seed: 8 vs 5 flagged, Wilcoxon p = 0.27. A re-seed alone shifts scores by +0.007 (p = 3e-4). tug_lib.score shows the mismatch arm also changes the prompt draw, so its upward shift is confounded with re-sampling. 'Doubles the false-positive rate' states as an effect a change that is non-significant and depends on the threshold; the 8/80 itself is right. Optional companion edit in §3.9(b), which is not wrong as it stands: append ', against 4/80 by construction under the correct sampler (4 newly flagged, none unflagged; exact McNemar p = 0.13); at the dev threshold (0.146) the FPR is 7/80 under the correct sampler and 8/80 under the mismatch.'
